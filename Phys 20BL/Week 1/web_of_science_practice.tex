\begin{ques}
    Find the oldest article in the American Journal of Physics measuring the speed of light.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item[a.] Proide its citation.
        \item[b.] Describe its measurement approach. 
    \end{itemize}
\end{ques}

\begin{proof}
    The oldest article in AJP I found that's about measuring the speed of light, is "A laboratory experiment on the velocity of light" in 1954.

    \begin{itemize}
        \item[a.] Citation:
        
        \hfil

        Palmer, Spratt, "A laboratory experiment on the velocity of light." Am. J. Phys. 22:481-485 (1954). https://doi.org/10.1119/1.1933791

        \hfil

        \item[b.] The measurement of speed of light here requires a light source, and a Kerr cell shutter to produce light pulses. Then, they used a half-silvered mirror to divide the light pulses into two beams (in orthogonal directions). With the two beams traveling different distances, let them get reflected back, and combined to be measured by the deterctor. Then, by adjusting the distance each beam travels, knowing the frequency of the light pulses, and teh light's behavior detected (as sinusoidal waves), these data can be used to directly calculate the speed of light.
    \end{itemize}
\end{proof}
